Season waiver: participation and risks

Team handball includes running, jumping, throwing, physical contact, practices, games and tournaments. Risks include collisions, falls, cuts, strains, sprains, fractures, concussions, heat illness, serious injury and, rarely, death. Risks can arise from participants, equipment, playing surfaces and other circumstances. Participation is voluntary. The participant agrees to follow safety instructions, report injuries and stop playing when unable to participate safely.

Release of liability

PLEASE READ: RELEASE OF CLAIMS, INCLUDING CLAIMS ARISING FROM ORDINARY NEGLIGENCE. To the extent permitted by Texas law, the signer releases Dallas Team Handball Club and its directors, officers, coaches, volunteers and event helpers from claims for injury or property loss arising from participation in club activities, INCLUDING CLAIMS CAUSED IN WHOLE OR IN PART BY THEIR OWN ORDINARY NEGLIGENCE. This release does not cover claims that cannot legally be released. A parent or guardian signs as to their own claims; whether any release affects a minor’s claims depends on applicable law.

Emergency care

I authorize emergency medical care for the participant when necessary and understand that the participant or parent/guardian remains responsible for treatment costs. This authorization does not replace any consent required by a medical provider or Texas law.

Concussion and conduct

Head injury symptoms can include headache, dizziness, confusion, nausea, vision changes and loss of consciousness. The participant will report a suspected concussion, leave play immediately and return only after appropriate medical clearance. Club staff may remove a player from an activity for safety.

Players must respect teammates, opponents, officials and staff. Fighting, threats, harassment, discrimination and impairment from alcohol or drugs during club activities may result in removal or suspension.

Photo and video choice

The club may take photos and videos during activities. Choose whether Dallas Team Handball Club may use identifiable images of the participant on its website, social media, promotional materials and videos. Declining does not prevent participation; notify staff if a group photo includes the participant.
